# Additional Information

## Interview Preparation Tips

### Technical Interview
- Review system design fundamentals (scalability, databases, caching)
- Practice Python coding problems (focus on clean, readable code)
- Be ready to discuss your past projects in detail
- Prepare questions about our architecture and challenges

### Behavioral Interview
- Prepare examples of leadership and mentorship
- Think about times you've handled technical disagreements
- Have stories about debugging complex issues
- Be ready to discuss your career goals

### What We Look For
- Strong technical skills and problem-solving ability
- Clear communication and collaboration
- Growth mindset and willingness to learn
- Alignment with our values and culture
- Passion for building great products

## What Happens After You Apply

1. **Application Review** (1-3 days)
   - Our recruiting team reviews your resume
   - We look for relevant experience and skills
   - Qualified candidates are contacted for phone screen

2. **Phone Screening** (30 minutes)
   - Brief conversation with recruiter
   - Discuss your background and interest
   - Overview of role and company
   - Answer your initial questions

3. **Take-Home Project** (3-4 hours)
   - Real-world coding challenge
   - Build a small API or solve a problem
   - Focus on code quality and design
   - You have 5 days to complete

4. **Technical Interview** (90 minutes)
   - System design discussion (45 min)
   - Live coding session (45 min)
   - Meet with senior engineers
   - Two-way conversation

5. **Team Fit Interview** (60 minutes)
   - Meet potential teammates
   - Discuss collaboration and culture
   - Learn about day-to-day work
   - Ask questions about the team

6. **Final Interview** (45 minutes)
   - Conversation with Engineering Director
   - Discuss vision and growth
   - Address any remaining questions
   - Mutual assessment of fit

7. **Offer & Negotiation**
   - Verbal offer within 2 days
   - Written offer with full details
   - Time to review and negotiate
   - Typically 1 week to decide
